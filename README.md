# AtliQ Hardware – Excel Sales Analysis

 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes AtliQ Hardware's sales and market performance using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis focuses on customer performance, country-level sales, and performance against targets to identify key business trends and gaps.

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze historical sales data and understand:

- Customer sales performance
- Market performance across countries
- Sales growth over time
- Performance against targets
- Areas requiring business attention

 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Formulas
- Conditional Formatting
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

 📈 Key Analysis

 Customer Performance

The customer analysis compares sales across 2019, 2020, and 2021 and highlights year-over-year changes.

Market Performance vs Target

The market analysis compares 2021 sales against targets across different countries.

Key observation:

- 2021 total sales: **$598.9M**
- Target shortfall: **$54.9M**
- Overall performance: **8.4% below target**
- India recorded the highest 2021 sales at **$161.3M**
 💡 Key Business Insights

- Sales increased significantly from 2019 to 2021.
- India was the largest market by 2021 sales.
- Overall sales remained below the 2021 target.
- Country-level performance showed varying gaps against targets.
